 The Alapahoochee River is a 14.4-mile-long (23.2 km) tributary of the Alapaha River in Georgia and Florida in the United States. Via the Alapaha and Suwannee rivers its waters flow to the Gulf of Mexico.The river rises on the boundary between Lowndes and Echols counties at the confluence of Grand Bay Creek and Mud Creek about 10 miles (16 km) southeast of Valdosta.
